About

Operations person. Builder by habit.

For nine years I've worked where the money actually moves — restaurant partnerships, buy-now-pay-later, and now card issuing. My day is clients, disputes, escalations, reconciliations and reports. 01 · DESKTOP APP

Ops Notifier — never miss a ticket

Operations teams live in Jira, but Jira waits for you to look. This desktop app watches it for you and pops up the moment something needs attention.

  • Desktop pop-ups for new tickets, replies and @mentions — client incidents alert the whole team
  • A warning 30 minutes before any ticket breaches its SLA
  • Team views: tickets with no reply for 24 hours, and tickets nobody owns
  • A monthly team scorecard: resolved, SLA met vs breached, fastest to close
  • A free AI that runs on the laptop, reads the ticket and suggests the next steps and a reply
  • One-click install on Mac and Windows; everyone sets their own check timings
PythonpywebviewJira REST APILocal AI · llama.cppHTML / CSS / JS
02 · DESKTOP APP

Operations Bulk Run — every bulk job, one app

Bulk operations used to mean a folder of separate scripts, a terminal and someone technical. I turned them into one app anyone in operations can run safely, in a few clicks.

  • 10 bulk tasks in one place: onboarding, fund loads & unloads, wallet transfers, monthly breakage, card termination
  • Checks everything before it runs — connection, file format, every row and the records themselves
  • If the internet drops, the run pauses and carries on by itself
  • A colour-coded Excel report and a full history of every run
PythonREST APIspandasExcel automationMongoDB
